📌 Founder Post #1 · Launch Guide Nov 2026 · Cebu City Launch

How the GISUGO launch works

GISUGO is starting in Cebu City. To get the board moving, GISUGO will post the first real gigs itself, in overlapping waves over the first weeks. These are real gigs with real GCash payment. They’re limited in number, and new ones appear gradually, not all at once, so check the feed often.

Wave 1

Creators and mapping

  • Creator partners come first (sponsored, and disclosed). They show Solicitor, Transporter, and Shopper gigs from start to finish on video, so you can see what the work looks like.
  • Solicitor gigs, mapping: walk assigned business districts and record each business with photos and location details.
Wave 2

Introductions

  • Transporter gigs: deliver flyers and printed materials to where they’re needed.
  • Solicitor gigs, introductions: visit the mapped businesses, introduce GISUGO, and hand over the flyer.
Wave 3

Live Shopping & Interviews

  • Shopper gigs: deliver welcome gifts to businesses we’ve introduced GISUGO to.
  • Consultation gigs: short interviews with trade and industry professionals.
🔄 The waves overlap. Later gigs start appearing before earlier ones finish.

What to expect

  • The first gigs are limited. GISUGO is brand new, so more people will apply than there are gigs. New gigs are posted gradually over the first weeks, so please be patient.
  • Apply, then carry on with your day. You can hold 10 applications at a time, and your tokens come back as soon as someone else is hired, so you can apply to something else right away. Not being picked for one gig says nothing about you.
  • Some gigs require filming and posting your work. They’re marked FOR VLOGGERS ONLY and include a short interview before hiring.
  • A complete profile and verification improve your chances.
  • Cebu City comes first. Other areas will show “No Gigs Yet” until we launch there.
  • You keep 100% of what you earn. GISUGO takes no cut, and payment goes straight to you by GCash.

Help GISUGO grow

  • Gigs come from customers, and more customers means more work for everyone. If you’d like to help, share GISUGO on Facebook or send it to someone who could use it: a business owner, a family with errands to run, or a relative abroad who needs something done in Cebu.
  • Sharing is optional and has no effect on who gets hired.

Thank you for being here at the start. Complete your profile, turn on notifications, and watch the feed.

— Peter J. Ang, Founder
